# Security Policy
> Status: Active | Maintainer: Security Lead | Last review: 2026-07-16
## Reporting
Email: security@tankwallet.dev (PGP preferred)
## Response Timeline
- Acknowledgement: 48h | Assessment: 7d | Fix: 90d (critical: 7d) | Disclosure: after fix + 90d
## Scope
In scope: web app, API, crypto, engines, plugins.
Out of scope: third-party deps, social engineering, physical attacks.
## Safe Harbor
Researchers acting in good faith will not face legal action.
